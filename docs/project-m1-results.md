**Model:** Ollama model used for testing

## Prompt 1: Marketing

**Prompt:**

Write a short Instagram post for Sweet Crumbs, a bakery in Lahore.  
Target university students. Use a friendly tone and include 1 call to action.

**AI answer:**

### Option 1: The "Study Break" Vibe

Stressed from those endless lectures and assignments?  You deserve a sugar rush!  Stop by Sweet Crumbs for the perfect study break treat to fuel your brain. 

Find us in Lahore! Tag your study buddy who owes you a treat!

#SweetCrumbsLahore #UniversityLife #StudyBreak #LahoreEats #SugarRush

### Option 2: The "Squad Hangout" Vibe

Looking for the perfect spot to chill with the squad?       From gooey brownies to aesthetic cupcakes, we’ve got the vibes (and the calories) you need! 

Come hang out at Sweet Crumbs and make your day a little sweeter. 

 DM us to pre-order your favorite box of treats! 
#SweetCrumbs #UniversityLife #SquadGoals #DessertLover #LahoreBakery

### Option 3: Short, Punchy & Sweet

Between classes?  Treat yourself to something delicious at Sweet Crumbs! The best spot in Lahore for your sweet tooth cravings. 

 Visit us today and grab a slice of happiness! 

#SweetCrumbsLahore #StudentLife #LahoreDesserts #TreatYourself

**Useful?** Yes, because the answer follows the requested friendly tone, targets university students, and includes calls to action.

**Anything wrong or made up?** Yes. The AI mentioned brownies, cupcakes, pre-orders, and other products that were not provided in the business details. It also called Sweet Crumbs "the best spot in Lahore," which is an unsupported claim.

## Prompt 2: Customer Service

**Prompt:**

You are the manager of Sweet Crumbs.  
Write a polite reply to a customer whose order arrived late.  
Apologise and offer a small discount.

**AI answer:**

Hi [Customer Name]! 

I am so sorry that your order arrived later than expected today. I know how disappointing it is to wait for your treats, and I really apologize for the inconvenience. 

We’d love to make it up to you! On your next visit or order, we’ll give you [10-20%] off as a small token of our apology. Just show this message to our staff or mention it when ordering. 

Thank you for being so patient with us!

Best, [Your Name]  
Manager, Sweet Crumbs

**Useful?** Yes, because it is polite, professional, apologises for the delay, and offers a discount as requested.

**Anything wrong or made up?** Yes. The AI invented a 10–20% discount range and added instructions about showing the message to staff. The prompt did not provide these details.

## Prompt 3: Growth

**Prompt:**

Suggest 3 low-cost AI tools a bakery in Lahore can use to attract university students with a budget of 15000 PKR per month.

**AI answer:**

The AI suggested:

1. Canva with Magic Studio AI for social media designs.
2. ChatGPT for marketing content, campaign ideas, and customer service.
3. CapCut for AI-powered short videos and social media content.

It also suggested a budget breakdown including Canva Pro, ChatGPT, CapCut, and Meta Ads.

**Useful?** Yes, because it provided 3 relevant AI tools and explained how a bakery could use them to attract university students. It also considered the 15,000 PKR monthly budget.

**Anything wrong or made up?** Some information may be inaccurate or outdated. The AI gave specific prices for Canva Pro and ChatGPT Plus and suggested a specific Meta Ads budget and targeting approach without verifying them. These prices and features should be checked before a real business spends money.

## Overall Test

The prompts successfully produced useful business-oriented AI responses. The testing also showed why hallucination awareness is important. The AI sometimes added business details, prices, product information, or claims that were not provided in the original prompts. For real client work, these details should be verified before publishing or spending money.
